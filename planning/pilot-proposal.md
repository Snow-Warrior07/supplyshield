# Permitted pilot and feedback protocol

Status: proposal ready; no external procurement reviewer, usability study, or operational pilot has yet been conducted. No user feedback or realized savings is fabricated.

Invite a procurement professional, faculty member, or campus purchasing coordinator with their permission. Begin with the public synthetic case. Ask them to identify the affected component, select a feasible recovery response, inspect remaining shortfall, and export the decision. Record task start/end, completion, misunderstandings, and missing inputs. Report participant count and sampling limitations alongside results.

| Stage | Work | Evidence / owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1: discovery | Understand planning horizon, purchasing approvals, supplier qualification, lead-time uncertainty and service priorities | Interview notes; reviewer and project owner |
| 2: sample walkthrough | Complete four tasks with synthetic inputs | Consented feedback log and task times |
| 3: data agreement | Define permission, anonymization, retention, data dictionary and access controls | Written scope and approved sample |
| 4: shadow planning | Run alongside the current process without executing purchases | Paired decisions; independent feasibility and human approvals |
| 5: limited rollout | Agree exceptions, ownership, training and manual fallback | Signed operational readiness review |
| 6: evaluate | Compare service, procurement spend, decision review time and failures against agreed baseline | Observed measurements with attribution and limits |

Potential integrations are read-only order/BOM/inventory/supplier-offer imports first. ERP writeback remains outside this prototype. Decision owners approve any actual sourcing change. Retain model/input versions and reviewed recommendations in a durable audit store before a private-data rollout.
